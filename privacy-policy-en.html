Rentville — Privacy Policy

Türkçe · English

Effective date: 17 November 2026
App: Rentville (com.bluelabsgames.rentville)
Developer: Blue Labs Games
Contact: bluelabsgames@gmail.com

Summary

The game itself asks you for nothing. There is no account to create, no name or email is requested, and the game contains no analytics of our own.

The game shows optional rewarded ads. These are served by Unity Ads, which collects your advertising ID, your approximate location, and technical details about your device. Because the Unity Ads software starts when the game launches, this happens even if you never watch a single ad. Details below.

What the game itself does not collect

The app's own code never collects, requests, or transmits any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information
  • GPS location, or any location permission
  • Contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information
  • In-game analytics or crash reports

What the ad software collects

Ads are served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). The data collected goes to Unity and is used for advertising:

Collected Purpose
Advertising IDSelecting ads, avoiding showing you the same ad repeatedly, fraud prevention
Approximate location (from IP address, country/region level)Showing ads relevant to your region
In-app interactions (ad shown, watched, skipped)Ad measurement and reporting
Device technical details (model, OS version, language, network type)Showing ads compatible with your device

Blue Labs Games never sees or stores any of this; the data goes directly to Unity. Unity's own policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Your ad choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Google → All services → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads). A reset gives you a new ID; once deleted, apps can no longer access it.
  • Turn off personalized ads: on older Android versions the same screen has an "Opt out of Ads Personalization" switch. With the ID deleted or personalization off, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.
  • Unity's own controls: during an ad, tap the "i" (information) icon to view Unity's privacy options, including requesting access to or deletion of the data Unity holds about your device.

Information stored on your device

So the game can pick up where you left off, the following is kept only in your device's own storage:

Stored Contents
Saved gameIn-game calendar, cash, buildings bought, flats, the fictional tenants the game generates and their satisfaction, renovations, event log
LanguageTurkish or English
Sound settingsMusic, ambience and effect volumes

Tenant names and faces in the game are generated at random by the game itself; they do not belong to real people.

This information:

  • is kept in the app's own private area (Android SharedPreferences),
  • is never sent to us or to any server of ours and is not shared with other apps,
  • may be included in Android's own device backup to your Google account if you have turned backup on in your phone's settings; that backup is handled by Google, and Blue Labs Games has no access to it,
  • is deleted entirely when you uninstall the app, or via Settings → Apps → Rentville → Storage → Clear data. "Restart" in the game also deletes the saved game.

It is not personal data and does not identify you.

Permissions

Every permission the app requests comes from the ad software:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDownloading ads and checking connectivity
AD_IDReading the advertising ID
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICSAndroid's ad measurement APIs
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICEKeeping the screen on while an ad video plays
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDLetting the ad software finish pending work after the device restarts

The game itself requests no permissions: it does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics tooling or tracking technology of ours. The only data shared with third parties goes through the ad software described above.

Third-party components

The game is built with the open-source Flutter and Flame libraries. Images and sounds come from public-domain (CC0) sources or were made for the game; tenant faces are generated on the device without going online (DiceBear, Avataaars artwork). This content ships inside the app and collects no data. Apart from the ad software, no component collects data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Because the ad software collects an advertising ID, we do not present the game as being aimed at children. If you believe your child has been using this game, please write to us at the address above.

Your rights regarding your data

Blue Labs Games does not hold any record that identifies you, so there is no account of ours to delete. You can delete the game progress held on your device by uninstalling the app, and reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings as described above.

Data deletion requests: you can still email us at bluelabsgames@gmail.com with the subject "Rentville data deletion". Step-by-step instructions: data-deletion.html. We will reply within 30 days, confirm that we hold no data about you, and help you send a deletion request to Unity for the advertising data it holds.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will be revised.
